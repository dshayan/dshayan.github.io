About

I lead product and teams at BimeBazar, one of Iran's largest insurtechs. When someone files a claim, they're already having a bad day. We built auto claims from scratch to make that process feel less like the day getting worse.

Before that I spent seven years at Divar, where most of Iran buys and sells things. I built the design team and developed its leaders, and shipped products that grew into businesses of their own. I created Sonnat, Iran's first open-source design system. Later I ran trust and safety, where automated moderation replaced the work of hundreds of reviewers.

Outside Divar, I've built products at startups across AI, travel, and ecommerce, and advised a design team in Iraq.

I came to this from civil engineering. I worked on a real building and a wastewater treatment pond in a lab. At some point it was obvious that software could reach millions where civil engineering reaches hundreds. That seemed worth doing instead.
